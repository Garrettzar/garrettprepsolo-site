What comes in the deck?

100 original SAT practice cards—50 Reading & Writing and 50 Math—plus 18 gameplay cards. Each practice card includes an answer, explanation, and clue on the back.

Who is it for?

High-school students preparing for the SAT, and the parents, friends, and family members who want to practice with them. The deck focuses on targeted question practice and complements full-length test preparation.

How do I study solo?

Draw a practice card, choose your answer, then flip it to review the answer, explanation, and clue. Revisit the questions you find challenging.

How does the family game work?

Up to six players answer questions for points, pass questions, and use BACK AT YOU! to reverse a passed question. Each player uses two double-sided answer cards to choose A, B, C, or D and reveal together.
